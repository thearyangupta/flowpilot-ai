Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of purchase if the service has not yet been delivered.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Customers should include their order number when requesting a refund.

Refund requests made after 7 days are not eligible under this policy.

If the service has already been delivered, the purchase is not eligible for a refund.
